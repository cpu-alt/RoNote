## What RoNote is

- RoNote reads your own Roblox trades with the session already open in your browser, shows

  accept, counter-offer, each confirmed twice).

## RoNote premium

- Neuronal lookups, Profiles and Deals are available to subscribers of the Neuronal
  Discord server. Access is checked by `neuronal.live` on every request and ends with the
  subscription.
- To use premium, you sign in with Discord or with your personal key, and verify **one**
  Roblox account, either with Sign in with Roblox (Roblox's official authorization page) or
  with a code in your public profile description. The linked account can be changed every
  7 days.
- Your key is personal: do not share or resell it. A shared key can be reset (`/key reset`)
  and access can be removed in case of abuse.
- "Sign in with Roblox" only asks Roblox for your user ID and username. RoNote never asks
  for your Roblox password and never sends your Roblox cookie anywhere.
