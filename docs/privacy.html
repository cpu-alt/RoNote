Privacy Policy : RoNote

Last updated: 15 November 2026 · Version 2.17.0

Version française

RoNote is a browser extension that shows desktop notifications about your Roblox trades. It is built to run entirely on your own computer.

In one sentence

RoNote does not send any of your data to anyone. There is no RoNote server, no account to create, no telemetry, no ads, no analytics, and nothing is ever sold or shared.

What RoNote reads

While you are signed in to Roblox, RoNote reads, from within your browser:

  • your trades : inbound, outbound, completed, declined: IDs, dates, status;
  • the items in those trades : names, IDs, thumbnails, values;
  • the public profile of the other player : username, display name, avatar;
  • your own username and Roblox ID, so it knows which account it is looking at;
  • your inventory, if you use the Portfolio tab or the revaluation alerts.

Where that data is stored

Only in your browser's local storage (storage.local), on your machine. In practice: your settings, trade tracking state, your account value history, and the event log.

That storage is local and never synced: RoNote deliberately avoids storage.sync, so nothing is uploaded to your Google account or to any other device.

To erase everything: uninstall the extension, or use Reset in the settings page. There is no copy anywhere else to delete.

The only network connections

By default, RoNote contacts exactly two domains, both declared in its manifest:

DomainWhyWhat is sent
*.roblox.com Read your trades, items, thumbnails and profiles Nothing beyond the request itself, using the Roblox session already present in your browser
*.rolimons.com Download the public item value table and, when you open an item, its public price history. With portfolio tracking on, read your public Rolimon's profile; when you open a player card, that player's public profile For the value table and item histories, nothing about you : the same public pages for every user. For profiles, a Roblox user ID, which is already public: yours for portfolio tracking, the other player's for a player card

Rolimon's can be turned off in the settings. Without it, RoNote falls back to the RAP reported by Roblox and contacts nothing but Roblox.

Neuronal lookups option (off by default). If you turn it on (Settings › Values), the browser asks you for access to neuronal.live, the Neuronal network website run by RoNote's author. When you then open a player's or an item's sheet, RoNote asks neuronal.live/ext/v1/ what the network knows about it: only that player's or item's Roblox ID is sent, with no cookie and nothing about you. The endpoint only reads: it never adds anyone to the network. Turning the option off gives the permission back.

Contribute to the Neuronal network option (off by default). If you turn it on (Settings › Values), with the same neuronal.live permission, RoNote sends neuronal.live/ext/v1/ two things, and only these: your account's presence (Roblox user ID, username, display name, extension version and the value of your inventory if portfolio tracking is on), at most every 6 hours, so the network can show you on its map as a RoNote user; and your completed trades, each sent once, exactly as Roblox returned them: the items on each side (item IDs, copy IDs, serial numbers), the Robux on each side, and your trade partner's Roblox user ID and username. Only trades whose detail RoNote has already read are sent: this option never adds a request to Roblox. No cookie, no token, nothing from your browsing. Turning the option off stops it at once.

The neuronal.live page can see RoNote. On neuronal.live (and nowhere else), the page can ask RoNote two things: whether it is installed, with its version and whether the contribution is on (yes or no), and to open RoNote's Neuronal settings. Nothing else is answered: no account, no trade, no setting. On Chrome the page can ask this as soon as RoNote is installed; on Firefox only once you have granted access to neuronal.live.

RoNote premium (optional, for Neuronal Discord subscribers). Neuronal lookups, Profiles and Deals are served by neuronal.live to subscribers only. To unlock them (Settings › Premium), you either Connect Discord or enter the key you received from the Neuronal Discord. Connect Discord opens Discord's own sign-in page through the browser (identity permission); Discord only gives Neuronal your Discord ID and name (identify scope: no e-mail, no server list, no messages). With a key, RoNote sends the key and the Roblox user ID and username of the account RoNote follows. To prove that Roblox account is yours, you paste a short code in your public Roblox profile description, which neuronal.live reads on the public profile page; RoNote never sends your Roblox session or cookie. Instead of the code, you can Sign in with Roblox: Roblox's own authorization page (OAuth 2.0, scopes openid and profile) tells neuronal.live your Roblox user ID and username, nothing else. RoNote then keeps a signed token (valid 24 h, renewed automatically) in its local storage and attaches it, with the Roblox user ID it follows, to premium requests only. Neuronal stores your Discord ID, the linked Roblox user ID and username, and your sessions (dates only), to check your subscription on every request. Sign out closes the session on the server and deletes the token. Premium is independent from the contribution option: signing in sends nothing about your trades.

Share proof (RoNote premium, Neuronal Elite only). On a completed trade, the proof mode shield opens a panel on the right that prepares the five lines Rolimons asks for in its #proofs channel (items, values, sender, receiver, date), which you can edit. Only when you click Send to my DMs, RoNote takes a screenshot of the tab, cuts it to the trade (and, if you tick it, the trade list with the other usernames hidden by proof mode), and sends the image and the five lines to neuronal.live with your premium token. The screenshot uses the activeTab permission, given by a click on RoNote's icon in that tab, or the optional screenshot permission if you allowed it once (Allow screenshots); without them, you can take it with Chrome's own tab sharing, which asks you first. Neuronal posts them back to you, in a Discord DM from its bot RoOp Tracker, and does not keep the image.

Automatic trade ads (off by default). If you turn them on (account menu › Automatic trade ads), RoNote posts the ad you composed (the items you offer, the items or tags you want) to the sites you ticked, at the interval you chose, never more than once every 15 minutes per site. It reuses the session you opened on each site in your browser: RoNote never asks for, stores or types a password, and reads no cookie. What is sent is the ad itself and your Roblox user ID: to api.rolimons.com (Rolimon's), and to routility.io (RoUtility) only after you allow that optional permission.

Without these options, no request goes to any other domain.

How your Roblox session is used

To read your trades, RoNote queries the Roblox API reusing the session already open in your browser : exactly as roblox.com itself does while you browse.

RoNote does not read, store or transmit your password. It does not copy your session cookie either: the cookie stays managed by the browser, which attaches it to Roblox requests on its own.

The only action that changes your account

RoNote can decline an inbound trade, cancel an outbound one and, since version 2.16.0, accept an inbound trade. Always on your explicit click, in two steps: a first click arms the button, a second one within 5 seconds confirms. Accepting transfers items and cannot be undone: that is why the second click is required. If Roblox asks for two-step verification, RoNote does not get around it: it opens the trade on roblox.com, where you finish yourself.

RoNote can also send a counter-offer to an inbound trade, composed in its editor: you pick the items and Robux on each side, the difference is simulated, and sending is confirmed with two clicks the same way. To offer you the items, RoNote reads both players' limiteds from inventory.roblox.com when you open the editor; nothing of it is kept. RoNote never creates a trade on its own: it only sends in reply to an inbound trade, on your click.

Children and minimum age

RoNote collects no data about you, whatever the user's age. Only the Neuronal lookups option, off by default, sends another player's or an item's public ID.
